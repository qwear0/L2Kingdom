# World, actors, movement and combat

Read this before changing spatial publication, native actions or effect timing. This covers live execution; retained cold combat models are approximations, not another source of native truth. See the [project map](README.md) and [bot runtime](bot-runtime.md).

## Ownership and reading order

| Anchor | Responsibility |
| --- | --- |
| `src/GameServer/World/World.js` | Session registration, actor/NPC/item presence and spatial queries. |
| `src/GameServer/Model/Creature.js`, `Model/Actor.js` | Creature values/state and publication-aware actor setters. |

| `src/GameServer/Effects/` | Effect storage, stacking, expiry and periodic execution. |

`src/NodeL2.js` initializes database/data/geodata/world before opening client listeners. The world owns presence, not every durable row associated with a character.

## Spatial publication and client visibility

1. `World.insertUser` establishes the current session binding; replacing an account retires old actor publication before socket teardown.
2. `Model/Actor.setLocXYZ` batches coordinate publication through `World.updateUserLocation`.
3. `World/CharacterLocationRuntime.js` binds the shared `CharacterLocationIndex.js` used for spatial lookup.
4. Actor lifecycle publication and movement are different events. A location update does not recreate the actor or emit all lifecycle-publication events.
5. `World/ActorVisibility.js` tracks real client recipients, sends appearance/removal and current movement information. `NpcVisibility.js` handles the NPC side. Internal bot waypoint/traffic-stop and equipment-appearance broadcasts select recipients before constructing one shared packet body, using `BotSession.dataSendToOthersLazy`; with no recipients, no body/framing or packet trace is built. The eager bot API shares the same visibility/tracking/send path. Authoritative movement and equipment/shot side effects still run; late entrants get current appearance/movement from visibility publication. Self-only equipment `itemsList` is omitted only for internal bot sessions. Human sessions, including server-controlled movement, retain eager self-delivery. No recipient set is cached across sends and no exact skipped-byte count is inferred for unbuilt packets.

Registration/current-actor identity guards prevent an old actor from publishing into a replacement session. Sending packets to “nearby users” must respect current online/visibility state, not an arbitrary persisted or observer actor collection.

The [observer](world-observer.md) builds its own cross-world projections and can include saved/offline details. That is not proof that a C4 client has received `CharInfo` or can interact with the actor.

## Movement and asynchronous paths

- `Actor/Generics/MoveTo.js` distinguishes ordinary client-driven player movement from authoritative waypoint movement. Explicitly owned player auto-fight actions reuse the native worker/waypoint path without bot identity, low-detail warps or town-errand fallback.
- `Actor/Generics/UpdatePosition.js` preserves reported Z and queues persistence. Spatial publication and SQLite durability are separate transitions.
- Vassals/companions and expensive routes can use `Geodata/PathfindingWorkerPool.js`. Results must still match actor identity, route generation, target and current location before application. Learned player-target resurrection and party resurrection scrolls explicitly allow dead-target approaches without bypassing those fences or online/corpse-lifecycle checks. Offensive actions do not gain this allowance.
- Cancellation invalidates the route generation. A late result must not resurrect an old move after a new command or character switch.
- Vassals explicitly bypass retained low-detail offscreen warp behavior; physical duty movement cannot be inferred from legacy population travel estimates.
- `GeodataEngine.walkSegment` traverses the cells crossed by the actual line, with reciprocal NSWE, source-floor continuity, 64-unit layer steps and both sides clear at exact corners. Path smoothing, direct-route fallback and server waypoint progression share it; intermediate height follows terrain rather than snapping an interpolated Z to a roof. Cells without layer data retain the engine's permissive fallback and use requested/interpolated Z, including authoritative waypoint and action stepping. Covered cells still follow the source floor and enforce layer continuity; fallback passage is not proof of authored collision.
- Native bot attack/skill and pickup interpolation also validate physical placement; blocked progression stops at the last accepted position with `MOVEMENT_BLOCKED`. Human client-driven movement is not replaced by this bot interpolation policy.
- Ground-loot queues can route around obstacles with bounded retries and a 30-second deadline before temporarily excluding an unreachable item. Companion catch-up/bring-to-leader teleports shrink unsafe formation offsets toward the leader; leader teleport synchronization falls back to the trusted leader destination rather than an unchecked offset. Native leader teleport coordinates are not globally height-snapped.
- Autonomous gathering has a separate [five-minute recovery watchdog](vassals.md#farming-stall-recovery); route retries alone cannot escape an isolated geodata island.

`Geodata/GeodataEngine.js` provides bounded, layer-aware pathfinding, height/NSWE checks and line-of-sight smoothing over region data. A* uses exact numeric keys for normal world cells/layers (string fallback outside that range) and a per-search cache of at most 65,536 cell queries keyed by XY and requested Z. The cache is released on return, not retained per bot; ordinary movement retains its node budgets, tie ordering and smoothing. Journey cost probes opt into `requireComplete` (node exhaustion throws `PATH_NODE_LIMIT`, not a no-route result) and `fastSmoothing` (exponentially increasing, terrain-validated shortcut probes instead of quadratic farthest-first smoothing). These options do not change ordinary movement callers. Direct-route fallback requires line of sight. Missing geodata can nevertheless permit passage and preserve supplied Z, so path success is not proof that every traversed region has valid collision data.

`Geodata/VirtualObstacles/index.js` overlays authored geometry. Resource preparation belongs to [runtime bootstrap](runtime.md); installed region validation is not comprehensive client-navigation acceptance.

## Native attack and skill flow

1. Network request handlers or BotAI select an intended action.
2. `Actor/Generics/AttackRequest.js`, `AttackExec.js`, `Automation.js` and `Actor/Attack.js` resolve approach, target and execution conditions.
3. Native range, resources, equipment, reuse, target restrictions and combat legality remain execution gates. A bot policy preference or LLM suggestion does not bypass them.
4. `Actor/Attack.js` schedules attack/cast stages and applies actual results through creature hit/effect owners. `Skills/C4SkillEffects.js` owns supported native skill effects.
5. Recipient-safe response paths publish animations/status. Request acceptance, launch animation and successful effect outcome are distinct events.

For connected players, a manual skill preserves an already active basic attack (including weapon approach and bow reload) against its previous target. Accepted casts retain that attack as a fallback across queued skills, including Spoil/Sweep; explicit queued movement or another action takes precedence. Resumption goes through `AttackRequest.js`, so current target legality, weapon range and attack restrictions still apply. A skill used without an active basic attack does not start one. Internal bots and controller-owned auto-fight actions do not acquire this fallback; cast interruption clears it.

Raid assistance is triggered by `Npc/Npc.js::enterCombatState`, not only by hit processing: acquiring a target through proximity, taunt or damage on a raid minion wakes its idle boss and the boss's other idle minions through `World/RaidBossMinionManager.js`. An engaged boss keeps its current hate-based encounter, but idle surviving or respawned minions still join. Assistance skips recursive redistribution, so each notification visits the group once. Hit processing retains the pre-death notification so a lethal first hit still alerts the group.

NPCs have their own timer-driven behavior. `World/Generics/RemoveNpc.js` separates corpse decay from reward/respawn handling; respawn timing starts at death, not when the corpse becomes invisible. Do not assume a single tick determines every stage.

`World/Generics/NpcDecay.js` owns an in-memory pending-corpse set per NPC container; `corpseDecayAt` remains the only deadline. Scheduling retains the individual timer and registers recovery work. The one-second reaper visits pending corpses only, with a one-time bootstrap for existing deadlines, isolated retries and a final malformed-corpse discard after three failed sweeps. Reaper stop/start retains pending work; `World.init` disposes the old container's corpse timers/session references before replacement. Timer identity and container guards prevent stale corpse callbacks from removing a replacement object.

Normal expiry, day/night batch removal, quest despawn, minion cleanup, Sweep and corpse-consuming summons share final decay cleanup: spawn removal, spatial/object/raid index removal, pending/timer/session release and best-effort `NpcVisibility.deleteKnownNpc` delivery. Sweep still initiates loot awards before removal and awaits settlement afterward; respawn scheduling is unchanged. Sweep and corpse summons no longer depend on a later timer to finish grid/client cleanup. Temporary pet travel unpublication does not retire the object or cancel its lifecycle.

Raid-boss deaths produce an English combat-log summary for human reward recipients in `Actor/Generics/NpcDied.js`: boss name, total EXP/SP actually granted to eligible characters (including vassals, excluding pets), and the rolled ground drops from `World/Generics/NpcRewards.js`. Each item type has one line with its total quantity, including separately spawned non-stackable instances; an empty roll says `No items dropped.` This is a drop summary, not confirmation of pickup or inventory custody, and does not include later Sweep or quest rewards.

`Npc/NpcReturnHome.js` limits ordinary static monsters and ordinary group minions to 2500 horizontal units from their actual spawn point. Leaving that radius, losing the target, breaking the existing 1500-unit target-distance envelope or exhausting the unreachable-target path budget clears combat/hate and starts a physical return through bounded asynchronous pathfinding. Return blocks reacquisition, damage and new effects, clears old effects and resets vitals/reward attribution on arrival, then applies normal spawn aggro grace. A failed home route is warned and retried every five seconds; it does not teleport or pretend arrival. There is no damage-inactivity timer. Raid bosses/minions, guards, summons/pets and dynamic quest NPCs are outside this policy.

`World/GroundItemIndex.js` maintains native ground object/cell lookup at SpawnItem, claim removal and world reset; PK/reward drops use that same spawn ingress. FetchItem and stale visibility rejection reuse it; pickup still owns global claim/recipient ordering. `World/BoundedCellQuery.js` and `CharacterLocationIndex.actorQuerySteps` yield empty-cell/candidate work for bounded [MCP pages](bot-mcp.md), without materializing the whole world. Ground coordinates remain immutable after spawn; any future ground movement/removal ingress must update the index. P4 [typed MCP commands](bot-mcp.md#typed-native-commands) also use native Spoil/Sweep, tracked plain self-item casts/travel, actor-position drops and mapped summon skills/pet operations. Concrete owners retain accepted casts and award/teardown tails through cancellation; no remote pickup, arbitrary drop XYZ, ground offensive cast or admin teleport is added.

`Npc.setLocXYZ` updates the NPC spatial index only when an already published actor changes cells. Late movement cannot republish a removed NPC. Local threat/target queries therefore follow moving NPCs rather than their original spawn sectors. Bot escape and defensive reassessment are described in [bot execution](bot-runtime.md#npc-escape-and-defensive-reassessment).

## Connected-player auto-fight

- Entrypoint: `.autofight` or `.menu` → `World/Generics/NpcBypasses/PlayerAutoFight.js`. Compact Hunt, Skills and Settings pages use stock HTML/HtmlKit, actor-bound epochs and consumed page revisions; no client patch or native-menu negotiation is required. Skills show paged native icons/names and immediate ON/OFF links, without an Apply step. Closing HTML does not stop a run; reopen it for Stop/status. There is no periodic forced popup.
- Setup requires an explicitly recorded zone center **and** rest point. Hunt accepts an integer radius of 1–6000 units through an edit field (default 1200), bounded by native client visibility. Saved radii retain their value. New session clears both points; Start/Stop preserves them. Rest-to-center and Start-to-center distances must not exceed 3000 units. There is no moving anchor, implicit point or fallback rest location.
- States are seeking, fighting, returning to rest, resting and returning to zone. A 250 ms timer performs bounded encounter/acquisition scans at most every 500 ms; healthy native movement/casts are retained. Player recovery uses saved integer enter/resume percentages (`1 <= enter < resume <= 100`), defaulting to HP 35/95% and MP 20/95%. MP matters only when an allowed learned skill has a positive native MP cost or the equipped bow's basic attack can consume MP; basic attacks without MP cost do not wait for mana. Party members use existing `RestPolicy` low/locked thresholds and role-dependent mana rules. Recovery survives defensive interruptions and blocks new party pulls before travel/sitting.
- New targets must be live ordinary monsters in the fixed circle, on connected terrain, without a foreign native claim/target. Height differences over 64 require geodata at both endpoints and a layer-aware line-of-sight check; a continuous slope may therefore rise more than 64 units overall without allowing a jump or cross-floor shortcut. Summons, raid bosses and protected minions are not acquired. Direct pressure on a party member or summon from an archer/mage or a monster with at least 200-unit physical range overrides the target/source acquisition height checks for retaliation, including a recorded hit within the last five seconds. Hate alone does not grant this exception. XY leashes, ordinary-monster/raid restrictions, physical route validation and native execution gates remain enforced; an inaccessible shooter is not permission to jump floors. Already engaged hunting targets may continue to radius + 200. An attack during travel/rest fixes a separate defence anchor at the player's interruption location, including outside the hunting zone. Targets, controlled actors and approach waypoints must remain within 400 units of that anchor; it is retained through adds and accepted native actions until the encounter ends. No new targets may be acquired during that defensive episode. An out-of-envelope or unsupported attacker keeps the encounter active and prevents sitting/new acquisition; status exposes the wait. These threat waits are recalculated each scan and clear once their cause disappears; they do not replace a latched fixed-route failure. No kill, return, target inspection or add expands the circle.
- Encounter completion includes current party/summon aggro and recent hits, native accepted claims, in-flight swings/casts and summon hit timers, not merely primary-target death or availability of an executable skill. Spatial queries are bounded around the player and members within 1500 units of the player, including at a rest point outside the hunting zone; dead/removed/replaced NPC objects cannot retain engagement by ID alone.
- Skills use a learned-ID whitelist, initially empty, with basic-attack-only ON and pickup OFF. Basic-attack-only retains the whitelist but disables all controller-issued casts, including healing, buffs, Spoil/Sweep, charge preparation and summon upkeep. Existing summons may still receive attack/follow commands. Structural edits require Stop; an allowed skill can be disabled during a run, invalidating pending choices/approaches and support caches without undoing launched effects. Learned rank changes retain ID permission; unsupported/unlearned IDs never become executable. The Settings page edits player HP/MP thresholds, basic-only and pickup while stopped; radius remains on Hunt. Disabling the last MP-dependent skill recomputes recovery applicability on the next tick. Unavailable saved IDs remain dormant and can be explicitly removed from Skills.
- Offensive selection reuses `BotCombatUtility`/`BotClassPolicy` with eligibility applied before scoring, including both charge preparation and its spender. Whitelisted Shield Stun (92) is a separate control action before damage selection, not a pure-debuff damage score; it requires an equipped shield, native resources/reuse/range and approach validation, and a target without an equivalent active stun or blocked-effect feedback. Native recipient expansion must be legal as a whole. Reuse, MP, weapon/items/charges, geometry and effect restrictions remain native; basic attacks are the fallback. Bows additionally need physical arrows and their native MP cost. No executable action produces a visible wait, not a fabricated success.
- `BotHealingPolicy` extracts the shared emergency/self-preservation/top-off decision from companion support. Player healing, routine Recharge and useful self/party buffs execute learned native requests only. Identified buffs are substitutes only by native identity or shared stack/benefit family, not stat overlap: Shield does not suppress Majesty, and ordinary buffs do not suppress separate songs/dances. `PartyPulling.supportProviders` includes the leader only during an eligible run; planning, caches and pull pauses use the same skill filter and native support reservations. Providers execute only their own actions. Routine support is in-range, idle and outside recovery; emergency allowed healing remains available at rest or during defence. The controller never calls `reconcileLoadout`, removes existing buffs or grants service buffs; companion loadout cleanup skips the active player.
- `SummonerTactics` reuses native servitor creation/material/lifetime rules, the existing pet-heal/buff IDs and explicit attack/follow commands. Necromancer corpse summons and Sweep use known encounter corpses; Sweep retains native spoil ownership/decay checks. Summon loops carry a separate run command identity; Stop releases only that identity, preserving a newer manual command and the summon itself. Creation/upkeep casts wait during recovery; existing attack/follow commands remain available. Native summon creation may still complete after Stop as an already accepted cast.
- Supported tactics do not imply every learned active skill is automated. Creation is limited to the existing three summoner roles and Necromancer corpse summons; preliminary-mage/mechanical/siege summon creation is not advertised. Cubics, offensive corpse explosions, standalone crowd-control/debuff rotations, summon special/siege skills, pet purchases/training/revival and class-specific movement rotations are not selected. No new class coverage or pet class is introduced.
- Optional pickup considers ground objects carrying the existing party-loot provenance for this leader within 200 units, inside the zone and on the same layer/line of sight, after an encounter and before new acquisition. Native geodata routes approach loot; the automatic pickup path cannot invoke the straight-line pickup interpolator from a distance. Recovery and defence interrupt collection; vanished/ineligible/unreachable items are skipped with bounded suppression. `Inventory/PickupCapacity.js` uses `InventoryLimits`, native mass/load and actual recipient stacks. Full player slots or 100% load latch a hunting pause while defence/recovery remain available; recipient-capacity rejection or an award failure also latches a visible pause until Stop/Start. Start rechecks player capacity. See [native pickup outcomes](economy.md#ground-pickup-and-award-outcomes).
- `PlayerAutoFightPolicy` temporarily constrains companion designated pulls, tank fallback acquisition and native offensive initiation without rewriting saved pull settings or detached vassal duties. `PartyAwareness.leaderCombatTargetId` reads the controller's committed combat target, not an inspected object. Native melee/skill expansion, including `Npc/SpoilSweep.js` utility casts, is checked as a whole before spending resources: an illegal polearm/AoE/Spoil Festival secondary victim rejects initiation. This is an initiation guarantee, not selective AoE immunity: an accepted cast retains native impact expansion, including world changes during flight, independently of recovery/Stop. Routine buffs, charge preparation and non-emergency Recharge are suppressed during recovery; native heals and defensive actions remain available.
- `PlayerActionOwner` fences run/action replacement through queues, asynchronous lookups, approaches and path callbacks. Automatic selection uses `selectionOnly` and execution retains its explicit target. Owned skills enter native execution without waiting for a future `ValidatePosition`. Existing equipment, reuse, resources, timing, interruption and effects remain authoritative.
- Target pursuit retains the native attack/skill target and refreshes its geodata approach instead of treating the original coordinate as the goal. A shift of at least 128 XY units or over 64 Z units refreshes an active path; reaching an obsolete endpoint also refreshes when the target shifted at least 8 XY units or over 8 Z units. Repaths are limited to once per second and do not cancel pending worker requests, reset the five-second no-progress watchdog or extend a pursuit beyond 90 seconds. An obsolete endpoint is refreshed rather than immediately rejecting the candidate for 30 seconds; actual path failures and expired progress/travel budgets still reject it. No unchecked `MoveToPawn` fallback bypasses owned routing.
- Owned movement uses the existing geodata worker and authoritative waypoints, without bot identity, low-detail warps or town-errand fallback. Worker routes are rejected before publication if longer than 6000 units, above 60 seconds estimated travel, outside the chase envelope, or containing a height change over 64 without a geodata-confirmed connected segment. Smoothed slope segments use the same layer-aware check as target eligibility rather than treating their total rise as a single cell step. Travel/no-progress deadlines are finite (5–90 seconds overall; 5 seconds without 16-unit progress). Arrival requires actual proximity/layer and target line of sight. A fixed rest/zone route gets one initial attempt and one retry, then a visible `path_blocked` wait until Stop/Start and optional point correction. Unreachable hunting candidates are suppressed for 30 seconds. Defence remains available in a wait; there is no alternate-point sitting. Existing missing-geodata limitations still apply; automated jumps/drops are not implemented.
- Rest timeout counts only safe, seated recovery at the stored point, not travel, combat, defensive interruptions or downtime. Six eligible hours without recovery stops with `recovery_timeout`.
- During owned movement, server progression owns XY; nearby passive reports may update heading/floor Z but cannot rewind/complete the route. Passive `StopMove` does not take control. Explicit manual movement/combat, second-click actions, pickup, sit/stand and summon commands stop the run before their normal action. First-click inspection remains selection-only. No special Esc behavior is promised.
- Start replaces unlaunched stored/queued requests. Stop removes only owned future requests/movement; it does **not** blanket-clear native attack timers. Already accepted swings/casts finish under native interruption rules, without automatic player repeats. Their completion can release a queued manual command and must not clear newer action flags. Stop retains points/preferences; death/teleport clear setup. Saved preferences survive logout/restart or actor replacement, but coordinates, active runs, targets, routes and timers never persist or automatically resume.
- Preferences load on the first window opening for each actor. Start and preference edits wait for initial load; missing rows use defaults, but invalid stored data or read failure visibly blocks Start until an explicit retry or replacement with defaults. Explicit edits save immutable snapshots through serialized `Database` operations. Save failure retains the in-memory edit with a visible warning/retry and blocks the next Start; a live skill-disable still takes effect immediately. Identity/lifecycle and edit revisions fence asynchronous results; page epoch/revision also fences delayed redraws. See [preference durability](persistence.md#connected-player-auto-fight-preferences).

Verification remains source/syntax only. P1–P4 implementation does not close the accepted C4-client evidence gap: HTML input/icons, saved preferences/relogin, selection, casts, physical routes, support coordination, summon cancellation, capacity/award outcomes, Stop/takeover and ordinary movement still need separately authorized runtime/client acceptance. Migration 69 is additive and runs on a separately authorized startup; no live migration is implied. Existing hard-coded 11-entry/unknown-12 assertions in `tests/test_native_menu.js` remain stale; tests are not changed or executed under the current authorization.

## Effects and saved status

- `Effects/EffectStore.js` owns normalized active effects, stacking, limits and absolute expiry. `packetEffects` captures one timestamp, prunes once and derives durations from that ordered snapshot, preserving rounding, toggle sentinel, packet limits and short-buff routing. `remainingMs` remains available for individual lookups; expiry observation is emitted by pruning, not repeated per serialized effect.
- `Effects/EffectRestrictions.js` acquires confusion candidates through the NPC grid and registered-actor spatial index, including vassals. The final filter retains the 600-unit 3D radius, self/dead exclusion and attackable-only variant; selection remains uniform over all eligible candidates, immediately and every six seconds. No new line-of-sight or aggression rule is added.
- `Skills/C4SkillEffects.js` admits cast effects before store insertion, timers or interruption. Raid bosses and actual raid minions (`minionBossObjectId`/`minionBossTemplateId`, not ordinary `minionLeader` groups) reject stun and physical mute on the actual recipient after reflection, including self-effect routes. Pure rejected control installs no effect/timer/icon; legal damage and independent target/aggro actions remain unchanged. Root, sleep, fear, silence, lethal, numeric trait resistance and over-level Raid Curse are not broadened by this policy.
- Compound raid effects retain their permitted payload: 361/362 keep defense reductions under a `stun_remainder` key and neutral category without stun impairment/abnormal flags; 1336/4215 keep magic mute/silence without physical mute. Skill identity, duration, stack family/order and cleanse identity are preserved without changing shared rule objects. `effectResisted = true` records rejected control; a non-null `effect`/`selfEffect` describes only an installed permitted remainder, with its legitimate icon, expiry and side effects. Native/AI effectiveness consumers use that actual outcome, so partial application is not total failure. Existing chance and stack rejection semantics are unchanged. This is cast admission, not a retroactive purge or generic restored/admin-state filter.
- `Effects/EffectTicker.js` owns periodic work, expiry processing, stat refresh and recipient-safe notifications.
- `Actor/CharacterStatus.js` saves/restores eligible unexpired non-debuff, non-toggle buffs, not every transient combat condition.
- Character entry restores effects before derived stats/vitals. Changing this order can expose incorrect initial status or duplicate effect application.
- Teardown must invalidate actions/timers and remove current publication rather than leaving callbacks attached to an obsolete actor.

## Update together

For movement/publication changes, inspect Model setters, world indexes, visibility recipients, character switching and asynchronous result fences together. For combat/effects, inspect native action gates, timing, effect ownership, bot feedback and status restoration. Changes to resources also affect [content/catalogue consumers](content.md). Syntax and observer snapshots do not certify real-client movement, collision, hit timing or combat correctness.
