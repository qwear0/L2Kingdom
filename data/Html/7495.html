Welcome to the Fortress of Resistance defense.
First profession and starting preparation
Buy consumables
Summon five loyal defenders
Start the fifteen attacking groups
Refresh status
Preparation is once per character: level at least 20, one complete high-D kit, 5,000 SSD or BSSD for its weapon and 10,000,000 Adena.
Summoning and starting the attack are separate shared actions, available once per server process.
Starting opens the fortress gates. You have 30 seconds to leave before the first group can spawn.
Attackers wait for route validation if it takes longer; later groups spawn 30 seconds apart.
Only the summoner can invite and command these defenders. Use ordinary party invitations.
Defenders wait after dismissal or logout. Dead defenders need resurrection; they never restart in town.
The squad is temporary. Its progress and ALL items, including items you give it, are lost on server restart.
There is no victory reward, defeat reset or second run. Defender deaths do not stop the remaining groups.
