# Cached vassal travel: P1 contract

Status: **contract and requirements only**. `travel-waypoints.json` is not loaded by the game. No road, component or local-access proof has been built. Existing journeys remain unchanged. See [content ownership](../../docs/content.md), [current journeys](../../docs/bot-runtime.md#journey-planning-catalogue) and [vassal duties](../../docs/vassals.md).

This contract fixes the P2 data/runtime boundary and the P3 consumer boundary. It is not authorization to implement later stages, generate the full network, or operate the server. Task progress and engineering evidence belong under ignored `tmp/plans/cached-vassal-travel/`.

- Selection of actual `TownServiceCatalog` rows for gatekeepers, sellers/generic merchants and warehouses, plus the fixed Castellan. Each selected service needs an approach anchor and a bounded local contact proof; an NPC coordinate itself is not a safe standing point.
- Authored accepted/rejected transit regions, waypoint candidates and directed trunk proposals. These arrays are initially empty. No candidate is silently inferred as an accepted road from a town-distance heuristic.

Each polygon is a separate coverage requirement, even where it overlaps another polygon. P2 adds proved data for its small slice without deleting the other requirements; P4 must settle every mandatory requirement.

Candidate records use `{id, kind, point:{locX,locY,locZ}, townKey?, areaId?, spawnId?, provenance}`. IDs are stable authored strings; generated component IDs are fingerprint-scoped, not persisted actor identity.

A connection proposal is `{id, from, to, transitRegionIds}`. It is directed. There is no implicit reverse edge, straight-line clearance, nearest-town shortcut or teleport edge in this road graph. Reversing geometry requires an independent native proof.

## Transit permission and exclusion

A transit region is `{id, polygon:[{locX,locY},...], minZ, maxZ, role, source, reason}`. `role` is `land_road`, `town_approach`, `farm_access` or `deny`. Bounds are finite; polygons have at least three vertices. Accepted region unions only permit proposed travel, never prove it. Every native cell crossing, including layer changes and smoothing shortcuts, must remain inside the accepted union and outside exclusions. Point-only checks and endpoint-only region checks are insufficient.

Default is **deny** outside authored accepted regions. Exclusion wins on overlap. `farm_access` is scoped to its `areaId`/`spawnId` and is not general inter-town transit. Final farm movement must also obey the assigned polygon and GatherAreas Z interval. Unknown/ambiguous safety metadata stays `unresolved/unsafe_transit`; it is not `unsupported` merely to make a build green.

Independently exclude:

- Dungeon cells/volumes from the exact `WorldAreaCatalog` predicates, including Seven Signs and detached underground layers; do not replace these predicates with surface bounding boxes.
- Raid bosses and raid minions identified by `RaidBossBalance.isRaidEntityTemplate` in registered spawn sources: fixed spawns exclude a **2000-unit XY radius** over their authored Z interval expanded by **256**; random spawns exclude their whole authored polygon expanded by **2000 XY / 256 Z**. A conservatively expanded bounding box is acceptable for rejection only. Unknown placement is unresolved, not safe.
- Explicit authored dangerous transit, inaccessible layers, missing/invalid region data, blocked virtual obstacles and water/sea gaps. This feature authors only reviewed land corridors; absence of a water tag is not permission to connect islands across water.

The 2000-unit raid buffer is a conservative routing exclusion, not a promise about native aggro range. Dynamic combat avoidance remains authoritative. No new general world-area classifier, safety registry or configuration framework is introduced.

## Exact layer components and local-access certificates

Current anchors: `GeodataEngine.getCellData`, `findPath`, `walkSegment`; `SpawnSurface.layers`, `supported`; `GatherAreas.containsZone`. Reuse the actual registered spawn-admission inputs/policy, including placement anchors and fixed coordinates, rather than treating every raw geodata layer as an intended mob floor. Native world cells are 16 units, with a 64-unit maximum floor step. Region coordinates are `(x >> 15) + 20`, `(y >> 15) + 18`.

1. Enumerate every 16-unit cell intersecting each authored polygon's bounding box, not random samples. Apply the actual polygon/Z admission rule to cell-centre surfaces. For non-centre spawn candidates, use the native point-to-cell connector and preserve the polygon boundary check; never promote a whole boundary cell to accepted territory. Any intersecting boundary fragment without a certified admitted centre/connector remains explicitly unresolved; centre sampling must not erase narrow spawn-admissible slivers. Retain every real, admitted, nonblocked layer, not just the layer nearest a polygon's `maxZ`. `SpawnSurface.layers` already decodes all native layers; reuse it rather than writing another L2J parser. Native equal-height layer selection/ties must resolve identically to `getCellData`; unaddressable ambiguous surfaces remain explicitly unresolved.
2. Require actual region bytes, valid offsets, real layers and virtual-obstacle clearance before admitting a node. Native movement's permissive `hasLayerData:false` fallback is not evidence. Fingerprint the complete relevant region set, not only startup's preloaded subset.
3. A graph node is `(cellX, cellY, nativeLayerZ)` within that polygon's admitted surfaces. Add the four cardinal native transitions only when `walkSegment(sourceCentre, adjacentCentre)` returns that exact destination layer, reciprocal NSWE/64-unit stepping pass, and the transition stays admitted. Do not invent an independent NSWE/collision engine. Diagonal smoothing must separately pass native `walkSegment` (both corner orders must agree). `SpawnSurface.walk` is not a substitute: its either-corner connectivity rule is weaker than movement's rule.
4. Compute strongly connected components with an iterative linear-time pass and a directed condensation graph. Preserve each direction independently; adjacent floors and overlapping XY are not connectivity. Process polygons in stable area/spawn order; component IDs use the smallest `(cellX,cellY,Z)` tuple plus input fingerprint. Keep isolated/one-way components as requirements, not discarded noise. Connectivity outside a polygon cannot silently merge its internal components.
5. For every spawn-admissible component, record separately (a) a directed trunk/access proof to an entry anchor, and (b) local-use coverage. One reached coordinate is insufficient. Partition large components into connected local patches using deterministic farthest-uncovered anchor refinement. Each patch must have at most **30000 layer nodes**, every admitted target must have an anchor-to-target path of at most **4096 units** within **2048 XY** of its anchor, and each required return direction must be proved separately. Use shared bounded traversal/distances, not one A* per potential mob. Preserve overlap memberships rather than assigning an overlapping cell to just one polygon.
6. Serialize exact cell/layer/component/patch membership as sorted row spans; a gap or a different floor never inherits a neighbour's component. Runtime lookup uses a prebuilt row/layer index, not a polygon flood fill. Native local search is restricted to the chosen certified patch/accepted connector domain, capped at the limits below; P2/P3 may add the narrow native admission/budget hooks needed for this, without changing other movement callers. Each process/worker may load one shared index; never clone the catalogue or patch geometry per request/bot.
7. A component requiring more than 8 directional entry anchors or 16 local patches is `unresolved/anchor_limit` until the authored network is refined. Geometry, time or work exhaustion leaves `unresolved/limit_reached`, not `unreachable`. `unsupported/missing_geodata`, `unsupported/unsafe_transit` and `unsupported/no_path_in_declared_domain` require respectively absent real inputs, demonstrated exclusions, or an exhausted complete permitted graph in the explicitly declared domain (not global physical disconnection); none is inferred from A* timeout or node exhaustion. Blocked/no-surface cells are accounted for in the decomposition summary, not fabricated as traversable components.

An entry/local certificate names its polygon, component, direction, anchor/patch IDs, proved node count, maximum path distance, covered layer-cell count and membership spans. Report excluded/no-surface/unresolved counts as well. If any admitted target layer is uncovered, the component is not `supported`. If resource limits stop enumeration, the **whole affected requirement** stays unresolved; enumerated prefixes do not establish completeness.

## Builder, file and loader boundary (P2)

Producer: a single offline generator, **not implemented in P1**. Consumer: one `JourneyRouteCatalog`, also not implemented. Output: `${L2NODE_RUNTIME_DIR || 'tmp'}/cache/journey-routes.json`; temporary output must be a sibling on the same filesystem.

The version-1 generated record contains `{schemaVersion, algorithmVersion, scope, inputFingerprint, checksum, inputs, coverage, waypoints, components, patches, membershipSpans, trunks, unresolved, buildStats}`:

- `scope` explicitly names `slice` or `full` and the selected slice requirement IDs. Both retain the complete 189-polygon/16-town requirement inventory. A supported slice cannot be advertised as full coverage.
- `coverage` has an entry for every polygon, resulting component and town/service access, with `supported|unsupported|unresolved`, reason, direction and certificate references. Absence, duplicate IDs, invalid references or outstanding required proofs prohibit a full supported result. A slice's unresolved requirements prohibit use outside its proved subset.
- `trunks` contain independent directed endpoint IDs, compact native-validated XYZ geometry, length, region/component/permission references and chunk offsets. Store distance, not actor-speed ETA, Adena, inventory, target availability, payment receipts or actor identity.
- `inputs` is a sorted SHA-256 identity manifest over exact authored waypoint JSON, GatherAreas definition, registered spawn/template and raid-minion inputs, service/NPC/teleport/restart sources, exclusion rules, virtual-obstacle exports, native movement/decoding/placement algorithms, generator/catalogue versions and bytes of **every relevant geodata file**, with explicit missing markers. Include admission constants. Hash loaded exports/bytes when `require` caching matters; a disk digest must never certify different loaded data. `GeodataEngine.inputIdentity()` alone covers only its loaded set and is not a whole-build fingerprint.
- `checksum` is SHA-256 of deterministic JSON (lexically sorted object keys, preserved array order) for the record excluding only `checksum`. Verify it at load, separately from input identity. Store shared adjacency/next-hop and bounded attachment/membership indexes once; speed, wallet and restriction exceptions use a small bounded topology search only at planning/replanning.
- Every geometry and certificate is validated before atomic rename. A failed build never overwrites the last valid file; preserve failure diagnostics separately with no implication that the old fingerprint is current. Loader checks size/schema/limits/references/fingerprint and publishes one immutable object only after all checks. Runtime snapshots use that immutable revision; no per-bot disk reads, hashing, lazy catalogue rebuild or runtime flood fill.
- Missing/stale/invalid files disable cached trunks with a precise diagnostic. Only a bounded permitted short route remains eligible; otherwise report blocked/deferred according to the finite stage contract. Never silently reactivate long-distance A* or use stale geometry. Startup and rebuild/publish commands are future P2 scope, not introduced here.

### Minimal catalogue interface

These are internal module contracts, not observer/network commands:

- `load(file, inputIdentity)` runs once before game readiness and returns `{status:'ready', revision, catalogue}` or `{status:'disabled', reason}`; it never builds/repairs data. Validation failures remain visible. The builder takes explicit `scope`/requirement IDs and the authored source path, returning coverage/stats before atomic publication.
- `membership(areaId, spawnId, point)` returns the exact admitted `{componentId, patchIds}` or an explicit missing/unsupported/unresolved result. Use actual point/floor admission against indexed spans; do not snap onto a different layer by tolerance. No geodata disk load or layer enumeration in this lookup.
- `attachments(point, direction, destinationComponent)` returns at most three compatible indexed candidates `{anchorId, componentId, patchId?, distanceLowerBound}`. These are candidates, not walk proofs; charge bounded native verification through the existing worker pool.
- `walkRoute(fromAnchorId, toAnchorId, exclusions)` returns an immutable edge-ID sequence and total verified length, or a precise coverage/permission/no-route-in-declared-graph result. Use precomputed shared next hops for static topology and bounded <=256-node/4096-edge traversal only for current exceptions; never perform geodata A* here.
- `fragment(edgeId, cursor)` returns a read-only handle/range limited to 64 vertices / 4096 units and `nextCursor`. MoveTo materializes only that fragment, tags detours separately and reports confirmed progress; retrieving a fragment does not advance the executor cursor or imply movement/arrival.

## Duty-stage budget and invalidation (P3)

`VassalOrders` owns one `run.travelStage` ledger: `{id, kind, state, submitted, failedSearches, reservedNodes, workerMs, connectorFailures, recoveryLaunches, resourceWaitMs, failure, startedAt}`. Its identity is based on actor/owner/order revision/vassal epoch plus a monotonic **stage sequence**, never coordinates, journey goal key, polygon, waypoint or route generation.

Stage kinds: `to_assigned_area` (including in-area transfer before useful farming), `to_town_services` (until the existing town/supplies/warehouse/done cycle completes), and `to_castellan`. An explicit new order/authority epoch creates a new ledger. A real completed business stage may create the next stage; returning to the same stage after unproductive recovery resumes its existing ledger. Only productive farming (native reward/XP evidence in the assigned area) or an explicit new order renews the failed farm-travel allowance. Position drift, movement progress, changed goal/area polygon/anchor, new iterator, revised plan, town arrival and watchdog recovery do not renew it.

`Journey.ensure` receives the ledger by reference and retains it across its existing goal-key changes. `Navigation.move` and `JourneyPathCosts.measure` accept the same ledger when called for this duty, charging before dispatch. Legacy non-duty callers keep existing behavior. `MoveTo` cannot run an uncharged town/worker fallback after a charged failure. Shared pending probes charge each participating stage's attempt, but actual CPU is not double-counted in aggregate metrics. Cancellation never refunds completed/reserved work. Queue admission failure does not spend nonexistent CPU but consumes observed waiting time; count each settled rejection once, not every AI tick. A failed submitted search consumes the shared six-failure allowance even when a new connector replaces its old per-connector counter; successful progress does not clear earlier failures. Observe wait time without counting offline gaps/combat/manual authority/casting, and do not reset it on retry.

When any relevant stage ceiling is reached, latch **`travel_work_exhausted`** with the narrower `work_budget_exhausted`, `resource_wait_exhausted` or `recovery_exhausted` reason and stop journey-owned work while retaining the order. Watchdog/target rotation cannot clear this terminal latch or launch another recall. A genuinely changed, validated catalogue fingerprint may permit one fresh attempt against the new route evidence; retry timers, worker availability, identical catalogue reloads, wallet changes and regained scrolls cannot. Capacity unavailability, runtime interruption and confirmed disconnected terrain are separate causes; no work-budget error becomes permanent `no_directed_path` evidence.

Invalidate active ownership immediately on authority/order/manual-control change, death, external teleport or an invalid navigation revision. Rejoin/replan on incompatible off-corridor displacement. **Speed** updates active-segment timing and future ETA without discarding valid geometry. **Wallet** changes revalidate unexecuted paid legs, but retain an affordable pinned itinerary; **scroll** changes matter only to an unstarted recall. Karma/action restrictions invalidate affected permissions. Replanning retains stage totals (apart from the explicitly different validated catalogue case above), the existing fare cap, spent-scroll/payment receipts and native-arrival latch. Never revisit a spent/uncertain native action or use stale geometry.

## Access-town and transport selection (P3)

Keep existing `JourneyPlanner`, `TravelRoutes`, `TripPayment`, `TownRespawn`, service contact and native arrival as owners. Add no second planner/payment/teleport subsystem.

1. Continue a valid pinned itinerary first; crossing the 12000-unit threshold or a moving local target is not a mode switch. For a new destination within 12000 XY, use bounded ordinary physical navigation without a separate full-route ETA probe. If it fails within its attempt policy, enter graph/recovery once under the same stage budget, never an alternating direct/recall loop.
2. When already in a recognized town (`TownTransitPolicy.townAt`), start from that town's compatible service/gate access; do not recall first. Otherwise try bounded direction-compatible road attachment. A useful attachment continues toward a destination access town without an avoidable recall. If neither applies, regional recall lands only at `TownRespawn.restartCoords`, with actual actor karma/restart rules, never at a requested city.
3. Select an **access town by a proved safe directed walking continuation to the destination component**, not by straight-line nearest-town distance. Score cached lengths/topology plus bounded local attachments. A source/start town and destination access town are different roles. Floran's walked access and disconnected islands remain represented explicitly.
4. From the source town compare safe cached walking with native executable gatekeeper trips plus their local approaches/final walking. Paid transport wins only if it saves **both >=30000 ms and >=20%** versus the available safe complete walking option. An unresolved connector is not evidence that no walking route exists: resolve it within budget or use a conservative lower bound only to prove savings, never as executable ETA. Among eligible verified alternatives within 20% of the fastest ETA, prefer lower fare, fewer hops, then stable key. Without a proved walking option, transport still needs all remaining policy/resource checks. Re-evaluate using current speed and funds; no inferred bidirectional sea/teleport land edges.
5. Before each **positive-price** hop actual Adena must be **>=50000**, and total itinerary fees must fit current funds and the retained `maxFare - paidFare`; free hops are exempt. Planning accounts for earlier debits when deriving the required starting wallet, not just the cheapest total fare. Being above 50000 does not authorize a later hop after funds drop below it. Native `GatekeeperService` still validates offers, contact, fees and physical arrival. Do not pre-debit or replay uncertain payments.
6. If no acceptable/affordable paid option exists, use a proved safe walking route. If no such land route exists, report an explicit transport/resource block; do not walk across a sea gap or substitute arbitrary teleportation.

Regional recall uses an owned Scroll of Escape when available; otherwise skill 2099 is a **300000 ms** regional unstuck cast. Both preserve stationary/combat/cast/owner fences and the native arrival callback. Unlike the scroll, unstuck consumes no fabricated item. Interruptions never imply successful arrival or reset stage totals. This is future P3 behavior; current `beginRegionalRecall` is scroll-only.

## Leg roles, chunk handoff and stall clocks (P3)

Keep action kind (`walk|gatekeeper|recall`) distinct from `purpose` (`transit|service_approach|final_access`) and `execution` (`local|cached`). Service approaches and paid transport do not acquire farm-final clocks merely because the underlying movement walks.

`JourneyExecutor` reports these roles plus catalogue revision, route/edge ID, edge/vertex/chunk cursor, chunk sequence, stage ID and stage-budget state. `VassalOrders.travelToGatherPoint` starts its short no-progress final-access clock only during **active final local approach** to the assigned polygon, never during cached transit, attachment/rejoin, recall/unstuck, combat, queue/capacity waits or service dwell. Watchdog pauses during healthy transit and suspended work, but checks the terminal budget latch **before** deciding to pause. Healthy vertex/chunk progress is not a new farming reward or a fresh recovery allowance.

Cached handoff: `JourneyExecutor` supplies an internal immutable catalogue geometry handle/range and bounded vertex/length limit to a narrow `MoveTo` entry; no arbitrary caller paths or new raw-path MCP/network API. Materialize only the bounded mutable fragment: existing town traffic uses `path.splice`, so never pass shared catalogue arrays into it. Track inserted steering points separately from confirmed catalogue progress. Do not call `actor.moveTo` separately for every vertex. Reuse `startPathMovement` timers, native `walkSegment` stepping, world publication, visibility, restriction/traffic checks and movement generation. Completion returns `{catalogueRevision, journeyGeneration, edgeId, chunkSequence, nextVertex, result}` exactly once; it schedules the next chunk only while owner/actor/order/manual-control/native-teleport/movement-generation fences remain current. No completion signal alone establishes final duty arrival: retain native location/Z and polygon checks.

Interruptions retain the last confirmed cursor but stop the old timers/chunk. After combat, directly resume only if the actual floor and safe remaining segment are still compatible; otherwise try the bounded forward rejoin window. Failed rejoin charges the same stage, then finitely replans or exhausts. Never jump to a later vertex by XY proximity or restart the trunk at vertex zero. Another order, manual control, death, logout or authority transfer invalidates all old chunks and pending callbacks. Payment/native teleport receipts survive interruption exactly as in the existing executor.
